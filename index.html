Rocket Lab acquires Iridium 🇺🇸
USA / New Zealand  |  Satcom M&A
$8B
Acquisition
M&ADual-useSatcomL-band Spectrum
Rocket Lab ($RKLB) announced on 29 June an $8B cash-and-stock deal to acquire Iridium Communications ($IRDM), partially financed by a $3.6B bridge loan from Deutsche Bank and Wells Fargo, with expected close in mid-2027. Iridium operates 66 LEO satellites using L-band spectrum - the default frequency for emergency services, aviation, GPS signals, and military communications - serving 2.5M+ customers globally. Rocket Lab gains a built-in moat in a market that Starlink and Amazon Leo cannot easily replicate. CEO Peter Beck described the L-band opportunity as "huge, huge" and confirmed plans to expand Iridium’s D2D and alternative PNT services. This is the largest acquisition in commercial NewSpace history.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
Rocket Lab is now the most vertically integrated commercial space company outside SpaceX - rockets, spacecraft, and a global satcom constellation. With a Swedish launch site also announced this week, their European ambitions are accelerating. Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027 is the right platform for Rocket Lab to engage ESA, European telecoms operators, and defence customers who will want to understand what the Iridium acquisition means for European spectrum and services access.

💰 Largest VC raise this week
Hongqing Technology 🇨🇳
China  |  LEO Satellite Manufacturing
$191M
Growth Round

Hongqing Technology - the satellite manufacturing arm of Chinese launch company Landspace - raised more than 1.3 billion yuan ($191M) on 2 July 2026, bringing total financing to over $368M. The round was led by the investment arms of China Construction Bank and ICBC alongside Jinpu Capital, with all five Chinese state banks participating. Hongqing filed the Honghu-3 constellation with the ITU in May 2024 - 10,000 satellites across 160 orbital planes - making it China’s third 10,000+ satellite megaconstellation alongside the national Guowang and Shanghai-backed Qianfan programs.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
China’s commercial space sector is maturing fast and actively seeking European credibility. Landspace operates the Zhuque-2 rocket and Hongqing is building constellation-scale manufacturing capacity. As Chinese companies target European spectrum filings, supply chain partnerships, and commercial customers, Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027 at ExCeL London puts them in the room with ESA, European operators, and procurement decision-makers. A strong candidate for future international exhibitor or delegation presence.

Firefly acquires Space-ng 🇺🇸
USA  |  Autonomous Navigation M&A
Undisclosed

Firefly Aerospace ($FLY) acquired Space-ng on 25 June 2026 - an AI-powered vision navigation and autonomous guidance systems provider. Financial terms were not disclosed. Space-ng’s navigation software guided Firefly’s Blue Ghost Mission 1 lunar descent and will now underpin Blue Ghost Missions 2, 3 and 4, the MoonFall drone mission to the lunar south pole, and DIU’s Sinequone Project for Space Domain Awareness operations in LEO. CEO Jason Kim flagged a "robust M&A pipeline" of further acquisitions to come.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
Firefly is building one of the most complete vertical stacks in commercial space - rockets, lunar landers, orbital transfer vehicles, and now autonomous AI navigation. Their Space Force Sinequone contract and lunar programme put them squarely at the intersection of commercial and defence space. With a Swedish launch site also announced this week, Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027 is the right moment for Firefly to build European institutional and commercial partnerships ahead of the Esrange launch programme.

Dublin-based Ubotica Technologies raised $11M in a Series A co-led by Ireland’s Act Venture Capital and Finland’s Greencode Ventures, with participation from Atlantic Bridge. The company has deployed its orbital AI on over 10 missions across 8 years of development. Its Live Maritime Intelligence platform uses hardware-agnostic edge AI to dynamically task satellites across optical, SAR, and RF modalities - delivering real-time maritime domain awareness. The raise comes as European governments scramble for better intelligence on undersea cable threats and offshore energy assets.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
Fresh funding, proven orbital AI technology, and a direct maritime security use case puts Ubotica on exactly the right growth trajectory for Space-Comm Expo Europe 2027. Their customer base - European and NATO governments, maritime operators, and coastal defence agencies - is the Space-Comm audience. A stand puts them in front of UK MOD, ESA contractors, and European satellite operators actively looking for AI-driven intelligence layers. Strong conference speaker candidate on dual-use EO and the future of maritime surveillance from orbit.
