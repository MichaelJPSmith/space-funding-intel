ElevationSpace 🇯🇵
Japan  |  In-Orbit Manufacturing & Re-entry
$40M
Series B

Japanese startup ElevationSpace secured $40 million in Series B funding on June 19, bringing total raised to $63.5 million. Investors include SPARX Asset Management, Beyond Next Ventures, NTT DOCOMO Ventures, Toyoda Gosei, and Dai Nippon Printing. The company develops the ELS-R platform - small uncrewed satellites that manufacture high-quality materials (including semiconductors) in microgravity and return them to Earth. Cheaper and more frequent than the ISS, ElevationSpace is positioning as the commercial successor for in-space manufacturing as the ISS approaches end-of-life.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
This Series B signals global expansion - they need European pharma, materials, and semiconductor partners seeking microgravity manufacturing access. Space-Comm 2027 connects them with ESA research groups, UK and EU in-space economy programmes, and commercial space station operators. Strong candidate for a speaking slot on in-orbit manufacturing as the ISS transitions.

Dawn Aerospace 🇳🇿
New Zealand / International  |  Reusable Space Transportation
$25M
Series B
Dual-useSpaceplaneOrbital Refueling
Dawn Aerospace closed a $25 million Series B on June 16, led by Balerion Space Ventures with participation from ANA Future Frontier Fund, Mana Ventures, and Green Eight Capital. Already the leading provider of non-toxic chemical propulsion with 200 thrusters on 50+ satellites, this round funds two milestones: the Aurora spaceplane (Mach 3.7, designed to cross the Karman line twice in one day) and a live in-orbit demonstration of their Loop satellite refueling technology in 2028. Commercial revenues have scaled from under $3M in FY22 to over $15M, 90%+ year-on-year growth.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
Dawn is actively expanding into the US and Europe with this raise - their propulsion tech is already on European satellites and their refueling roadmap is directly relevant to European operators extending asset life. Space-Comm puts them in front of satellite prime contractors, ESA supply chain, and UK defence customers at exactly the right growth stage.

Katalyst Space raised $12 million in a Series A announced June 16, led by Geodesic Capital with Fortitude Ventures. The funding supports NEXUS - their robotic spacecraft for GEO satellite servicing - with a first mission scheduled to launch in 2027 on Ariane 6. The raise follows Katalyst's first-of-a-kind mission to boost NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory: their LINK robotic spacecraft launches June 27. The company is extending satellite servicing from LEO across multiple orbits, with both government and commercial customers.
Space-Comm Outreach Angle
A mission launching on Ariane 6 and GEO servicing ambitions make Katalyst naturally aligned with European satellite infrastructure. Their customers - GEO operators extending asset life - are exactly the Space-Comm audience.
